About Makena Paint Co.

We Don't Just Paint Walls. We Understand Colour.

We are Makena Paint Co — a Pretoria North painting contractor with over 8 years of experience transforming homes and commercial spaces. We started as part of Makena Building Group, bringing the same discipline and care from construction to every paint brush.

Every project starts with a conversation — about what you want, what you need, and what will look best in your space. We give honest advice, quality materials, and professional execution. Whether it's a single feature wall or a 50-unit commercial project, the process is the same.

We're proud to be trusted by homeowners across Pretoria North, Rosslyn, Soshanguve, Montañana, Annlin, Sinoville and surrounding areas.

Frequently
Asked Questions.

A typical single-storey home exterior takes 5–7 working days. Interior painting varies — a single room can be done in 2 days, a full home interior in 10–15 working days. We provide a clear timeline in our initial quote so you know what to expect.

We supply all paint and materials as part of our service — we pass trade pricing to you with no mark-up on materials. For hardware supply clients, we sell paint in bulk at trade rates without the labour component.

Walls, ceilings, doors, window frames, trims, fascias, gutters, roofs, fences and garages. We work with plastered surfaces, Rhinolite, wood and metal. Proper surface preparation — cleaning, sanding, priming — is always included.

Gamazine application averages R65–R95 per square metre, depending on ceiling condition and accessibility. We offer a free on-site assessment where we measure the space and give you an exact quote — no rough estimates.

Yes. Interior work carries a 2-year warranty against peeling, flaking and bubbling caused by poor workmanship. Gamazine ceilings have a 1-year warranty. If something is wrong due to our work, we come back and fix it at no cost.

Absolutely. We have access to the full Dulux, Plascon and other major brand colour libraries. Bring us a swatch, a photo, or a description — we can match it precisely or help you choose from thousands of existing colours.
